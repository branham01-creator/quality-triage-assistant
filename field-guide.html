THE FIRST
RESPONSE.

A shift supervisor needs to know which work to pause. Materials needs to know which lot to hold. The Quality Triage Assistant helps a quality lead give both teams a clear starting point when an inspection finds a defect.

INTAKE

Start with the observed facts.

Record the part, lot, failed characteristic, and number actually inspected. Then account for each location where the suspect material could be, including stock, work in process, and customer shipments. A failed sample identifies a problem. The remaining pieces from its lot still need to be traced.

CONTAINMENT

Give each action an owner.

In the bracket exercise, Materials identifies the stock location and Operations traces the 25 installed brackets to their assemblies. Quality reconciles the counts and checks the hold evidence. If shipment records show that pieces left the site, the designated customer interface assesses the applicable notification requirements.

HANDOFF

Separate findings from open questions.

A management update should state the issue, exposure, containment status, cause status, and next action. Mark a proposed hold as pending until someone verifies it. Keep the cause and recovery date open until evidence supports them. The fictional guide supports triage. Product disposition stays with authorized personnel.

Worked example

SIX VALVES
SHIPPED.

Forty valves are on hold. Six more from lot V-12 shipped yesterday. Which facts belong in the first management update?

Sample management update
Issue
Two valves from lot V-12 failed leak testing.
Exposure
Six valves from the same lot shipped yesterday. Their condition is unknown.
Containment
Forty on-site valves are quarantined. The shipped units still need to be traced.
Cause & recovery
Cause is unknown. A recovery date has yet to be established.
Next action
Materials traces the six shipments. Quality and the customer interface review the evidence and confirm who will report back.
